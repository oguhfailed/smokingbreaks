💚 Consider quitting slowly with NRT

Nicotine Replacement Therapy — patches, gum, lozenges, inhalators — lets you step down your nicotine dose gradually, reducing cravings without the thousands of toxic chemicals in smoke.

You don't have to quit cold turkey. Fewer breaks each week, paired with NRT, is a proven path to freedom. Talk to your GP or pharmacist — they want to help.
